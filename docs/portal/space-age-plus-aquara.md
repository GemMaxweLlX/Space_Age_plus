# Space Age+ Aquara

Aquara, moon of Gleba: an ocean world with small islands and Gleba's biosphere.

- **Algae:** water towers grow green, deep and bioluminescent algae that spoil; sea salt preserves them.
- **Storms and tornadoes:** storms slow machines and spawn tornadoes that crawl towards your buildings. Storm shields protect, storm anchors pull tornadoes in and store their energy.
- **Aquatic pentapods:** Ripple and Surf nests (capturable) and the solitary Abyss.
- Underwater drill, venom gland chemistry, storm forecaster, carapace armor.

Requires Factorio 2.1 with Space Age. Source and in-game tests: https://github.com/GemMaxweLlX/Space_Age_plus
