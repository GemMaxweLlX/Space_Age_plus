# Space Age+ Core

Shared mechanics for the Space Age+ planets. Works on its own and is required by every Space Age+ planet mod.

**Oxygen.** Vulcanus, Fulgora, Gleba and Aquilo (and the Space Age+ planets) have no breathable air. Without modular armor you suffocate at once; modular armor gives a 30-second emergency system, oxygen tank equipment adds more. Build distributors for breathable zones, collectors and generators for oxygen.

**Hidden technology tree.** Most technologies start hidden. Find data cards in ship wrecks and ancient vaults, decipher them for a choice of technologies. Card quality brings better-quality technologies whose buildings get productivity, speed and efficiency bonuses. The logic lab complex is a slow, chancy backup.

**Planet exploration.** A telescope on Nauvis unlocks generation 1 satellites; after ten launches the generation 2 satellite surveys planets and unlocks travel to them.

**Also:** hive capture recalibration for Space Age+ planets, high-grade fluid systems, steam turbine Mk2 that returns 90% of its water, 500-unit cisterns, ancient vaults with alien technologies, and an engineer's diary (J) that comments on your progress.

Every mechanic has its own setting, including separate hardcore options.

Requires Factorio 2.1 with Space Age. Source and in-game tests: https://github.com/GemMaxweLlX/Space_Age_plus
