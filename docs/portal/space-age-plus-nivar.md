# Space Age+ Nivar

Nivar, moon of Aquilo: an ice desert colder than Aquilo, with nothing to mine on the surface.

- **Caves:** a dark layer under the ice with nivalite, cryolite and dark ice. An elevator shares storage between the levels and takes you down; the deep drill brings cave resources up.
- **Cryostorms** freeze machines in stages. Cryo heaters, cryo domes, insulating foundation and insulated walls protect them.
- **Creatures:** Blizzard packs in storms, Ice guardians, and blind cave creatures that wake when a storm rages above.
- Cryo pipes and cave belts that never freeze, cave armor, cryo armor.

Requires Factorio 2.1 with Space Age. Source and in-game tests: https://github.com/GemMaxweLlX/Space_Age_plus
