# Space Age+ Debris

What you throw off a space platform does not vanish. It becomes debris on that stretch of the route and comes back as chunks your asteroid collectors catch and crushers recycle. Debris is shared: what one platform collects is gone for everyone.

After 20 rocket launches from a planet, every further launch leaves wreckage in orbit. Orbital wreckage sometimes falls on the planet, damaging what it hits.

Near Space Age+ planets the debris changes: electrified around Echaris, heated around Solaris, covered in biofilm around Aquara, frozen around Nivar.

Requires Factorio 2.1 with Space Age. Source and in-game tests: https://github.com/GemMaxweLlX/Space_Age_plus
