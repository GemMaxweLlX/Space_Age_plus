# Space Age+ Solaris

Solaris is the planet closest to the Sun: lava plains, endless daylight and no atmosphere (oxygen only from generators).

- **Coronal mass ejections** strike the surface. Umbrellas intercept them, ray catchers turn them into energy or heat.
- **Overheating:** machines heat up in stages and stop or break without cooling radiators.
- **Resources:** magmatic ore, heat-resistant crystals, volcanic gas from volcanic vents, molten metal.
- **Power:** a ray catcher heats a super boiler; the mega turbine returns 90% of its steam as water.
- **Creatures:** Scorch and Magmar nests (capturable) and the solitary Molten.
- Cargo pods cannot land here until you research the improved delivery capsule.

Requires Factorio 2.1 with Space Age. Source and in-game tests: https://github.com/GemMaxweLlX/Space_Age_plus
