# Space Age+ Echaris

Echaris, moon of Fulgora: the legacy of the Fulgorans, still running without its masters.

- **Permanent magnetic storm:** machines run at 50-70% and robots are damaged outside storm stabilizers; Robots Mk2 are storm-proof.
- **Mechanites:** five robot types and control servers that cannot be captured. They patrol their zones, call reinforcements, EMP your turrets and reclaim zones you leave without a force barrier.
- **Capture centers** hold data cards of their type once their servers are destroyed.
- **Technologies:** force barrier, stealth suit, silent capture protocol, Mechanite hacking with EMP grenades, modular reactor, Mk2 versions of many vanilla buildings and more.

Requires Factorio 2.1 with Space Age. Source and in-game tests: https://github.com/GemMaxweLlX/Space_Age_plus
