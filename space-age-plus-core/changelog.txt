Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-11-09
  Major Features:
    - Oxygen system: collector, generator, distributor, armor tanks, emergency system.
    - Planet exploration: telescope, generation 1 and 2 satellites.
    - Hidden technology tree (on by default): data cards, decipherer, logic lab complex, data wrecks.
    - Quality versions of building technologies with productivity, speed and efficiency bonuses (I3).
    - Engineer's diary (J): background choice, 72 entries from the design document triggered by game events, company logs in wrecks.
    - Ancient vaults with alien data cards and encrypted data; three alien technologies; decryption in the decipherer.
    - Diary entries for vaults and decryption, autonomous base, production deadlock, asteroid hits, first delivery, first Nauvis capture.
    - Tips and tricks section "Space Age+" explaining every mechanic; planet briefings unlock with their discovery.
    - Settings preset: Custom / Casual / Standard / Hardcore.
    - API for other mods (docs/api.md), with keep_visible and set_tech_category for the hidden tree.
    - Planet science packs: each made only on its planet, needed by the Space Age+ technologies tied to it.
    - Ancient race dungeons: an entrance next to each vault; one dungeon per planet with its own layout (grid, lava chain, ring, flooded islands, ice caves), doors with power and key locks, guardians, the Keeper, caches with alien cards and an ancient core; four new alien technologies.
    - Story of the ancient race: each dungeon's ancient core opens a chapter in the diary; the Ancient nexus (cores and science packs of every planet) offers three endings — Awakening, Inheritance, The seal — with epilogues and an optional victory screen.
